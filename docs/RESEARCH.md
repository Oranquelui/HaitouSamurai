## Behavioral Interpretation

Core user job:

```txt
I want dividend income that feels safe, but I do not want to get trapped by high yield, dividend cuts, or unclear fundamentals.
```

Key behavioral drivers:

- Loss aversion: investors dislike selling principal to fund life expenses.
- Mental accounting: dividends feel like spendable income while principal feels protected.
- Regret avoidance: investors want to avoid obvious yield traps and public mistakes.
- Cognitive ease: checklist and scorecard formats reduce research burden.
- Social proof: X-friendly scorecards and ranking visuals travel better than long analysis.
